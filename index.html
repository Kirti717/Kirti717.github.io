Who We Are

Sharma LLC is a technical consulting partner focused on helping organizations innovate faster, build strong technical foundations, and reduce technical risk. We work with startup founders, CTOs, R&D leaders, and engineering executives in biotech, semiconductors, optics, and microelectronics.
